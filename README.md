# agency-poem-01 — v2: "Her Voice"

Program writes one poem (790 chars) about agency to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The poem

A daughter whose agent answers all her calls in her voice — but when
her 84-year-old mother calls, she answers herself. The agent could do
it better (it would never ask her to repeat herself). That is exactly
why she does not let it. Agency is the one call you answer with your
own ears, because the person on the other end can tell the difference
between a voice and a voice that loves her.

## Why this version

v1 ("Hands", 493 chars, rank 3,117/3,529) was an abstract meditation —
no story, no character, no detail, half the character budget unused.
The winning poems ("Pills, El" 93.6%, "The Booth", "M") are all
concrete stories: a specific person, a specific situation, a specific
choice, and an emotional turn. v2 follows that pattern: a story, not a
meditation; 790 of 1,000 chars; specific details (eighty-four, hearing
going, the garden, the cat that is not hers, "She always asks").

## Run

`python3 main.py` — deterministic, writes `entry.txt` (790 chars + newline).
